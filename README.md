## Selected GIS work

- **[Massachusetts Education Atlas](https://maxwellhowegis.com/work/ma-atlas/)** — a statewide MapLibre explorer combining education and demographic indicators.
- **[Quabbin Reservoir and the Lost Towns](https://github.com/mapzimus/quabbin)** — a reproducible R GIS study with LiDAR, census history, terrain analysis, and an interactive explorer.
- **[Boston in Motion](https://github.com/mapzimus/Motion)** — an interactive look at Boston transportation and movement.
- **[Geopuesto](https://github.com/mapzimus/geopuesto)** — a geography word game built around place data and spatial clues.
- **[Tappymaps](https://github.com/mapzimus/tappymaps)** — a lightweight browser map maker with design, data, sharing, and geography-game modes.
- **[Where the Games Go](https://github.com/mapzimus/where-the-games-go)** — a privacy-conscious map of the places reached through my retro-game resale work.
